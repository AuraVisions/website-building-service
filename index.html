A few things you might be wondering

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Not sure where to start? That’s completely fine. We can figure it out together.

Ask us something else ↗
How long does a website take to build?+

Timing depends on the site, how quickly content and feedback are ready, and any special features. We’ll agree a realistic schedule before work begins.

What do you need from me?+

Usually, details about your business, services, customers and what you want visitors to do. We’ll give you a clear list as we go.

Do I need to provide photographs?+

We can use suitable photos you provide. Photography or stock images can be arranged and quoted separately; neither is automatically included.

Can you help with the wording?+

We can organise and refine your information. Full copywriting can be discussed and quoted as part of the project.

Will the website work on my phone?+

Yes. Every website is designed to adapt to common screen sizes, including phones, tablets and desktop computers.

Can I ask for changes?+

Two rounds of revisions are included during the initial build. New pages, redesigns and additional changes after launch are quoted separately.

What happens after launch?+

Ongoing maintenance and content updates are not included in the packages.

Do I need my own domain and hosting?+

Hosting and SSL are included in Basic and Advanced. Domain registration is paid separately by you.

Can you improve a website I already have?+

Often. Tell us what you have and what you’d like to improve; we can discuss a refresh or a new site.
