A few things you might be wondering

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Not sure where to start? That’s completely fine. We can figure it out together.

Ask us something else ↗
How long does a website take to build?+

It depends on the size of the site, how quickly content and feedback come together, and any special features. We’ll talk through a realistic schedule before the project starts.

What do you need from me?+

Usually, a bit about your business, the services you offer, who you serve and what you want visitors to do. We’ll give you a clear list of what’s needed as we go.

Do I need to provide photographs?+

If you have images that show your business well, we can discuss using them. Photography or stock image licensing can be arranged separately; it isn’t automatically included.

Can you help with the wording?+

We can help organise and refine the information you provide. Full copywriting can be discussed and quoted as part of the project scope.

Will the website work on my phone?+

Yes. Every website is designed to adapt to common screen sizes, including phones, tablets and desktop computers.

Can I ask for changes?+

Yes. The included review and revision rounds are agreed in your package before we begin. Additional work can be discussed if your needs change.

What happens after launch?+

We’ll hand over the completed site and explain the agreed next steps. Ongoing updates or maintenance can be discussed separately if you need them.

Do I need my own domain and hosting?+

A domain name and a place to host the site are needed. They’re separate costs unless your written proposal specifically says they’re included. We can help you understand the options.

Can you improve a website I already have?+

Often, yes. Tell us what you have and what you’d like to improve, and we can see whether a refresh or a new build makes more sense.
